Installation (Minecraft Bedrock)

  1. Download RAAJYA.mcaddon on the device where Minecraft Bedrock is installed.
  2. Open the file. Minecraft will launch and import both packs (you may need to choose Open with Minecraft on Android/iOS, or double-click on Windows; on macOS, open it with Minecraft).
  3. Create a new world (or edit an existing one) and activate both the RAAJYA Behavior Pack and the RAAJYA Resource Pack. The packs are linked, so activating one offers the other.
  4. No experiments/experimental toggles are required on the target engine (custom items, blocks, entities and the stable Script API are all out of preview).
  5. Load the world. In Creative, find the Commander Stone and the Royal Soldier Spawner.

Implemented features

  • Commander Stone — non-stackable royal token with a unique, persistent Commander ID (e.g. RAAJYA-7F31A9C2).
  • Royal Soldier Spawner — reusable, placeable custom block; crafted from 9 Diamond Blocks in a full 3×3.
  • Royal Guard — a custom humanoid soldier entity with its own model, geometry, texture and render controller.
  • Recruitment — interact with a spawner holding a Commander Stone + 5 Emerald Blocks to recruit exactly one guard.
  • Ownership — every guard stores the Commander ID of the stone used; survives world save/reload and works for multiple players.
  • Safety — resource checks, exact consumption (never the stone or spawner), safe-spawn search, rollback on error, interaction cooldown.

Quick start (in-game)

  1. Give yourself a Commander Stone (Creative inventory) — interacting with a spawner assigns its persistent ID.
  2. Get 9 Diamond Blocks and craft a Royal Soldier Spawner (all 9 crafting slots).
  3. Place the spawner and keep 5 Emerald Blocks in your inventory.
  4. Right-click / interact with the spawner to recruit a Royal Guard. Repeat as many times as you like.

Advanced command authorisation, combat/patrol AI and a Barracks are intentionally out of scope for this initial phase.
